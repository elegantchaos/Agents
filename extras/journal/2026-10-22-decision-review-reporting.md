# Report Decision Deviations Only

Sam asked to remove exhaustive decision-compliance tables from review reports.

The review skill still requires reading and assessing every decision. The assessment
stays in internal working notes. Reports and journals include only deviations, relevant
conflicts or uncertainty, and unresolved coverage gaps, unless an inventory is explicitly
requested. Both report templates and the completion checklist follow the same rule.

Baseline plugin version: 0.8.11 in both runtime manifests.
