# 2026-10-14

## Review skill

- Sam runs adversarial reviews: one of Claude or Codex makes a PR and the other reviews it. Claude's reviews are usually thorough; Codex's are often superficial. Sam wants broad reviews: correctness, conformance to our standards, design quality, and the change's effect on the wider codebase, including obsolete code, duplication, possible simplification, and design clarity.
- Added `baseline:review`. Its codebase-effect pass turns each concern into concrete searches, and its report has fixed sections that must record what was checked even when nothing was found. The aim is to stop a reviewer stopping at the diff or reporting a bare "no issues". It points at `baseline:standards` and the project's decisions rather than restating them, and tells agents to prefer it over the built-in `/code-review` and `/review` commands.
- The refresh skill now includes `baseline:review` by default for software repositories, and this repository's `AGENTS.md` lists it. Both baseline manifests are bumped to 0.5.0.
