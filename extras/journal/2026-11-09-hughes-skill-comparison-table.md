# Kyle Hughes’s Skills Compared with Agents

Comparison of all 29 skills at [Hughes’s `ea711c1` revision](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/tree/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426) with the Agents sources at `af20cae`. The ten Universal skills come first, followed by the nineteen Apple Platform skills. The proposed changes come from a source comparison. See the [research journal](2026-11-09-hughes-skill-comparison.md) for priorities, reviewer roles and layout options.

| Kyle’s skill | What it does | Our corresponding skills | What we could take |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [brainstorming-design-specs](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/brainstorming-design-specs/SKILL.md) | Turns an ambiguous request into a design someone can implement. | `baseline:records`, `baseline:standards` | Research unresolved choices and ask only questions whose answers would change the design. Finish with a concrete acceptance bar. |
| [compiling-agent-goals](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/compiling-agent-goals/SKILL.md) | Turns a spec or issue into a finite goal an agent can work on across turns. | No direct match. `baseline:records` covers the source material. | For explicit persistent-goal work, define the observable conditions for finishing, the evidence required, and what counts as a blocker. Keep the spec as supporting context. |
| [delivering-changes](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/delivering-changes/SKILL.md) | Takes a change from the initial brief through implementation, verification, cleanup and PR handoff. | `baseline:git`, `baseline:standards`, `baseline:review`, `swift:validation` | Give `baseline:git` a short end-to-end route that carries decisions and check results forward. Make it clear where to resume after a required manual check and which checks belong to review, merge or release. |
| [designing-documents](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/designing-documents/SKILL.md) | Organises a document around what readers need to understand, decide, do or look up. | `baseline:records` | Add a reader-task check. | [reviewing-code](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/reviewing-code/SKILL.md) | Finds concrete correctness, security, concurrency and compatibility defects. | `baseline:review` | For consequential changes, try a focused correctness reviewer who gets the exact revision, scope and evidence and returns line-specific findings. Fold those findings into our broader review of decisions and effects across the codebase. |
| [simplifying-code](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/simplifying-code/SKILL.md) | Removes needless complexity and reuses existing code while preserving behaviour. | `baseline:standards`, `baseline:review` | Add a scoped cleanup pass for substantial changes. | [verifying-invariants](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/verifying-invariants/SKILL.md) | Checks whether an invalid value or state can actually be created or reached. | `baseline:standards`, `baseline:review`, `swift:language` | State the guarantee, trace the paths that could bypass it, and choose a check that could disprove it. | [writing-escalation-briefs](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/writing-escalation-briefs/SKILL.md) | Packages a stuck problem or difficult judgment for an expert who has no prior context. | No direct match. `baseline:standards` covers debugging. | Add a short handoff recipe with the reproduction, decisive evidence, failed attempts, and the exact question whose answer would change the next step. |
| [writing-technical-prose](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/universal/skills/writing-technical-prose/SKILL.md) | Writes and edits developer-facing docs, READMEs and comments. | `baseline:records`, `baseline:standards` | Check that wording-only edits preserve each material claim. | [analyzing-app-store-business-data](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/apple-platform/skills/analyzing-app-store-business-data/SKILL.md) | Analyses App Store revenue, subscriptions, downloads and reviews. | None. | Defer until there’s an App Store reporting task. | [configuring-ios-content](https://github.com/kylehughes/agent-plugins-for-making-good-software/blob/ea711c19c39c4cdaee5943e5a7d3065cb2ae2426/plugins/apple-platform/skills/configuring-ios-content/SKILL.md) | Configures UIKit list cells, including cells that host SwiftUI, and handles their state changes. | No direct match. `swift:swiftui` is adjacent. | Keep as a reference for UIKit interoperability work.
