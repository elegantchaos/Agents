# RP1: Roadmap And Work Items Trial

- **Status:** Started
- **Depends on:** nothing
- **Sources:** [Agents roadmap scope](../journal/2026-11-05-agents-roadmap-scope.md), [Roadmap work items](../journal/2026-11-17-roadmap-work-items.md), [Bookish roadmap reorganisation](https://github.com/elegantchaos/Bookish/blob/main/Extras/Journal/2026-10-27-roadmap-reorganisation.md)

## Finish Condition

Sam decides, from experience in Bookish and Agents, whether the records skill should describe a roadmap with work items. If yes, the guidance is added to the records skill and installed; if not, the reason is journalled and both projects keep or drop the pattern on their own terms.

## Notes

This replaces the earlier "evaluate upkeep on real work" and "refine shared guidance" slices. Evidence to collect:

- whether a fresh chat can pick the next task from the roadmap and items alone, without reading the journal or reviews;
- whether items stay one PR in size, or need splitting too often to be worth the files;
- whether keeping record updates in the same PR as the work holds up in practice.

Bookish's version adds a release plan between its roadmap and its items. Agents has no releases, so its roadmap lists the items directly.
